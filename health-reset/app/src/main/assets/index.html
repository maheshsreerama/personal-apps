    The three food rules
    1. Protein first. Build the meal around it, then add the rest.
    2. Half the plate is vegetables. Volume without calories.
    3. Nothing with calories gets drunk. Sugar in chai, juice, soft drinks — that's the silent 300 kcal.

    Cooking oil — the hidden calorie

    1 tbsp = 120 kcal. Measure it with an actual spoon for two weeks until you can eyeball it. This is the single most common reason a plan stalls in an Indian kitchen.

    If today is a wreck
